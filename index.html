Government Subsidy — PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana

The Government of India scheme provides Central Financial Assistance (CFA) for eligible residential rooftop solar systems. The official scheme structure provides 60% of benchmark cost for the first 2 kW, 40% of benchmark cost for an additional 1 kW, and no additional CFA beyond 3 kW for the standard residential CFA structure. The commonly published maximum CFA is ₹78,000 for a 3 kW system.

How the Solar Installation Works

  1. Check electricity connection and rooftop suitability.
  2. Register/apply through the official PM Surya Ghar process where eligible.
  3. Select an eligible vendor and finalize the system.
  4. Vendor installs the rooftop solar system.
  5. DISCOM inspection/verification and applicable meter/agreement process.
  6. Eligible CFA/subsidy is processed according to the scheme rules.
